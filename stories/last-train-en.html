The Last Train

The last train left at 03:17. But the light in the window was still on.

Only a clock, tea in a paper cup, and a man who could not remember whom he had come to see remained on the platform. In his pocket was a ticket with no destination.

When the train disappeared, the lamp above the bench blinked twice. Someone was still waiting for it to arrive.
